129,056 orders a second in. 645,280 records a second out. On a laptop.

One Kafka broker, four cores, one docker compose command. Exactly-once delivery, with every output verified against the real topics.

Claude Code built the whole thing — the pipeline, the test suite, the measurement harness, the CI, the dashboards and the deck. It cost about six hours of my own time at the keyboard.

[LEAD IMAGE: docs/img/scaling-linear.png]

The pipeline turns block trades into positions and market value. Every order fans out to five records: four account-side allocations plus one symbol-side position. So 129,056 orders in per second is 645,280 records written per second.

And it gets there linearly. Orders in, per second:

• 1 core — 30,505/sec
• 2 cores — 65,721/sec, 2.15× the previous
• 4 cores — 129,056/sec, 1.96× the previous

Four times the cores, 4.23× the throughput. Nothing varies but the cores.

Flink 1.20, Kafka 3.9, Java 17, all in Docker.
