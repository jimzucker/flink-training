A Flink pipeline that scales linearly: double the cores, double the throughput

Measured, not asserted. On a laptop, against a single Kafka broker.

Claude Code built the whole thing — the pipeline, the tests, the measurement harness, the CI, the dashboards and the deck. It cost about six hours of my own time at the keyboard.

[LEAD IMAGE: docs/img/scaling-linear.png]

Block trades in, per second:

• 1 core — 30,505/sec
• 2 cores — 65,721/sec, 2.15× the previous
• 4 cores — 129,056/sec, 1.96× the previous

Four times the cores, 4.23× the throughput — and since every order fans out to five records, that is 645,280 records a second written back to Kafka.
