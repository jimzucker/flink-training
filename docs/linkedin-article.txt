Capacity you can plan: double the cores, double the throughput

We built a trading pipeline on Apache Flink and Kafka — block trades in, positions and market value out — and measured exactly what each additional core buys.

[LEAD IMAGE: docs/img/scaling-linear.png]

Block trades handled, per second:

• 1 core — 30,505
• 2 cores — 65,721
• 4 cores — 129,056

Four cores do 4.23× the work of one. No surprises, no cliff. That is a capacity model you can budget against — you know what the next core buys before you buy it.

And it is real work: every trade fans out to five downstream updates, so at four cores the pipeline is writing 645,280 records a second.

All of it on a laptop, against a single Kafka broker.

And Claude Code built the whole thing: the pipeline, the tests, the monitoring, the build and release path, and the benchmark harness that produced these numbers. Thirteen reviewed steps, about 20 hours end to end — roughly six of them mine.
