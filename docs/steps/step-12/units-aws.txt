what a unit of parallelism buys -- AWS  (2026-08-29)
a unit is one core and one degree of parallelism, bought together
c7i.4xlarge client (8 physical cores / 16 vCPU), 2 x kafka.m5.large, RF=1
8 partitions, checkpoint 10000ms, 60s window after 120s warm-up
120000000 orders queued, measured draining the backlog with the producer stopped

   units    orders/sec   vs prev    flink   broker   back-pr    runs
                                    cores    cores    essure
       1         43538         -     1.00     0.30    20.40%    2 (43974, 43101)
       2         64106     1.47x     2.00     0.39    20.55%    2 (64669, 63543)
       4        104912     1.64x     3.99     0.45    34.90%    1
       8        181133     1.73x     7.99     0.62    65.40%    1

one order becomes five records: four account-side allocations plus one
symbol-side position, so 181133 orders/sec is 905665 records/sec.

the 1- and 2-unit cases were each measured twice and agree within 2%.
the 4- and 8-unit cases were measured once.
