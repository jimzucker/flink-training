## 2026-08-26 01:56 UTC

**Prompt**

> whats rf=1

**Response**

**Replication factor** — how many copies of each partition Kafka keeps, on different brokers.

- **RF=1** — one copy. Each partition lives on exactly one broker. Lose that broker and you lose that partition's data outright.
- **RF=3** — three copies. One leader takes the writes, two followers replicate it. Lose a broker and a follower is promoted; nothing is lost.

Production Kafka is essentially always RF=3, usually with `min.insync.replicas=2` so a write isn't acknowledged until at least two copies have it. That's what makes Kafka durable.

##### Why RF=1 here

**Because RF=3 would triple the write volume and destroy the comparison.** Every record written would have to be copied to two more brokers before `acks=all` returns. The pipeline already writes 5 records per order — at RF=3 that becomes 15 broker-side writes per order. The measurement would then be dominated by replication traffic, and we'd have no idea whether an AWS-vs-laptop difference came from the hardware or from the durability setting.

The local broker was RF=1 (it was a single broker — it had no choice). So matching that on AWS is what keeps the comparison to one variable.

##### What it costs us

It makes this cluster **not durable**. Three brokers, but any one of them dying takes data with it. That's an acceptable trade for a throughput measurement that lives under an hour and whose data is synthetic — and it would be indefensible for anything real.

It's flagged in the Terraform as such, and I raised it earlier as the thing to change if the AWS number came out high; you confirmed RF=1 is what you want, which is the right call for this run.

**The genuinely interesting follow-up** is running the same drain at RF=3 afterwards. The difference between the two is a measured answer to "what does durability actually cost this pipeline?" — which is a more useful number for a deck than the raw AWS-vs-laptop delta, because it's a decision someone actually has to make. The cluster is already sized for it; it's one more drain.
