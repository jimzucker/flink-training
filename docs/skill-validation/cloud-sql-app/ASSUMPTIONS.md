# Assumptions — mine, not approved

- The app is deliberately small (one INSERT, one output per input): its job is to prove the harness
  runs the same app on both platforms, not to be the business case.
- On the laptop only completeness and the tiny proof need to pass: they are what the cloud's
  "local first" gate reads. The laptop suite is not run.
- Laptop backlogs: 20,000,000 for completeness, 150,000,000 for the tiny proof, guessed high.
