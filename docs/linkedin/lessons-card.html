Six rules for letting AI build something.

I let Claude build the same system 29 times over 24 days. 156 test runs, 55.9 hours of machine time — and 30 of those hours produced work I had to throw away. Autonomous execution still needs experienced engineering supervision.

1Stop at the first failure.
2Analyze before you experiment.
3Unit test every fix.
4When the data changes, check the whole system.
5Confirm tests started—and keep monitoring them.
6Make important rules enforceable.
